A bookkeeper records what happened: transactions categorized, accounts reconciled, reports generated. A fractional controller goes further: building the processes, analyzing those reports, identifying what the numbers mean for your business, and giving you actionable insights. If you are just starting out or need clean books, start with bookkeeping. If you are growing, making hiring decisions, or need someone who can tell you why your cash keeps disappearing, you need a controller.

After you sign on, here is what happens in the first week: Day 1, we have a 30-minute kickoff call where I learn your business structure, current tools, and priorities. Days 2 and 3, I get read-only access to your books and do a diagnostic review, identifying gaps, errors, and anything that needs immediate attention. By Day 5, I send you a plain-English summary of what I found and a 30-day action plan. From there we move into the regular working rhythm, whether that is weekly check-ins, monthly close, or async updates, whatever fits your workflow.
